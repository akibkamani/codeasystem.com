# CodeASystem Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2026

## No accounts or forms

This is a static site with no login and no direct contact form. CodeASystem does not collect personal data through account creation or on-site submissions.

## Analytics and ads

Optional analytics or advertising scripts may be enabled to understand traffic and support the project. If enabled, third-party providers may use cookies under their own policies.

## External platforms

Links to social channels and external platforms are governed by those platforms' privacy policies.

## Data requests

Because there is no user account system, there may be limited data available to access, export or delete.

## Official channels

For questions, contact CodeASystem through its official social channels.
