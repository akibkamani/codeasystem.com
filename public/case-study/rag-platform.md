# RAG Platform

Search, retrieve and answer from your sources with clear citations.

## The challenge

A standard chatbot can sound confident without showing where an answer came from. That makes it hard for teams to trust it when decisions depend on internal information.

## How we would build it

We build a retrieval layer that indexes your documents, selects relevant material for each question, and returns answers with clear citations and source excerpts.

## How it helps your organisation

Teams can use AI for research and decision support while checking the evidence themselves. It brings more consistency to answers across operations, sales and client-facing work.
