# AI Knowledge Assistant

[All case studies](https://codeasystem.com/case-study.md)

Give your team a secure way to chat with company documents and data.

## The challenge

Policies, project files and past decisions are spread across drives, wikis and inboxes. People waste time hunting for answers or asking the same questions again.

## How we would build it

We connect the approved knowledge sources, set access rules by team or role, and create an assistant that answers in plain language with links back to the original source.

## How it helps your organisation

Your organisation gets faster onboarding, fewer interruptions for subject experts, and a reliable place for staff to find the current answer without exposing sensitive information.
