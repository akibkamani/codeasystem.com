# Case studies

# Practical starting points for complex work.

These are the kinds of systems we build when teams need useful software, not a long slide deck. Each approach starts with the real workflow, then adapts to your data, people and goals.

1. [AI Knowledge Assistant](https://codeasystem.com/case-study/ai-knowledge-assistant.md): Give your team a secure way to chat with company documents and data. Knowledge, search, answers.
2. [RAG Platform](https://codeasystem.com/case-study/rag-platform.md): Search, retrieve, and answer from your sources with clear citations. RAG, retrieval, citations.
3. [AI Customer Support](https://codeasystem.com/case-study/ai-customer-support.md): Resolve common questions faster using your own company knowledge. Support, automation, AI.
4. [SaaS MVP](https://codeasystem.com/case-study/saas-mvp.md): Take a product from idea through a usable, deployed first version. Product, payments, launch.
5. [Custom Backend](https://codeasystem.com/case-study/custom-backend.md): APIs, data, jobs, integrations and infrastructure built to last. API, database, infrastructure.
6. [Workflow Automation](https://codeasystem.com/case-study/workflow-automation.md): Replace repetitive steps with dependable automated workflows. Operations, integrations, jobs.
