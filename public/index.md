# CodeASystem

CodeASystem is an independent software studio building practical AI products, backend systems and custom software around the work that matters.

## What we build

- AI and GenAI: RAG applications, AI assistants, document intelligence and AI automation.
- Backend systems: FastAPI and Python services, PostgreSQL, payments, authentication and realtime systems.
- SaaS and MVPs: Subscription products, customer portals, admin systems and internal tools.
- Web and growth: Business websites, landing pages, technical SEO and content systems.

## How we work

1. Describe the project, including the problem, required features, timing and budget.
2. Review requirements asynchronously by email or chat.
3. Receive a clear scope, timeline, price and milestones.
4. Build against milestones, review together and deploy the result.
