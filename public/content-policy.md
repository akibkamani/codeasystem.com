# Content Policy for Crawlers and AI Agents

Last updated: August 17, 2026

CodeASystem welcomes responsible crawling of its public website by search engines, research tools and AI agents.

## Permitted use

You may crawl, index, summarise and cite publicly available CodeASystem pages. When citing or describing content from the site, link to the original canonical page and identify CodeASystem as the source.

## Conditions

- Follow the directives in [robots.txt](https://codeasystem.com/robots.txt) and use reasonable request rates.
- Keep claims in context. Summaries should not imply endorsement or a client relationship that the source does not state.
- Label AI-generated material as generated where that would reasonably affect how a reader interprets it.
- Do not collect personal data from the website or combine site content with personal data for profiling.

## Restricted use

Do not republish substantial portions of the website, create a competing mirror, misrepresent the source, or use the content to train, fine-tune or evaluate an AI model without prior written permission from CodeASystem.

The site content is informational and does not constitute legal, financial, security or other professional advice. The [Terms of Use](https://codeasystem.com/terms.md) and [Privacy Policy](https://codeasystem.com/privacy.md) also apply.
